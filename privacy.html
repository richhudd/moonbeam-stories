Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Moonbeam Stories is a parent-controlled service for creating personalised children’s stories. This policy explains what information we use, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Who controls the information?

Moonbeam Stories is currently operated in the United Kingdom by the individual trading as Moonbeam Stories. For privacy questions, contact privacy@moonbeamstories.co.uk.

Information we use

We may process the parent’s email address and account identifiers; child profile details entered by the parent, such as a first name or nickname, age, interests, dislikes and story preferences; generated stories and saved-story information; payment and credit records; and technical/security information needed to operate the service and prevent abuse.

Children’s information

Moonbeam Stories is designed to be set up and controlled by a parent or guardian, not by children independently. Please provide only the child information needed to personalise a story. Do not enter sensitive information about a child that is not necessary for the service.

Child photographs

If you choose to add a child photograph, Moonbeam keeps the selected photo in your browser/device storage rather than storing it in the Moonbeam cloud database. When you ask Moonbeam to create an illustration using the photo, the image is sent securely to our AI image provider for that generation request. Removing the photo from Moonbeam removes the locally stored copy used by the app on that device. Generated illustrations may still form part of stories you have created or saved.

How we use information

We use information to create and personalise stories and illustrations, provide accounts and saved stories, process prepaid story-credit purchases, provide narration, protect the introductory offer from repeated claims, prevent abuse, diagnose faults, and operate and improve the service.

Service providers

Moonbeam uses specialist providers to operate the service. These currently include OpenAI for story, illustration and narration generation; Supabase for authentication and application data; Stripe for payments; Resend for account email delivery; and Vercel for hosting and server-side application functions. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own applicable terms and privacy commitments.

Payments

Card details are entered into Stripe Checkout and are not stored by Moonbeam Stories. Moonbeam receives payment status, transaction identifiers and information needed to award the correct story credits.

Introductory-offer abuse prevention

To stop repeated free-trial claims, Moonbeam uses pseudonymous hashes derived from a browser/device identifier and network information. Raw device identifiers and raw IP addresses are not stored in the introductory-trial claims table. These signals are used for fraud and abuse prevention rather than advertising.

How long we keep information

Account, child-profile, saved-story, credit and transaction information may be kept while the account is active and for as long afterwards as reasonably necessary for security, accounting, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Local child-photo data remains on the device until it is removed, browser data is cleared, or the device/browser storage is otherwise deleted.

Your choices and rights

You can remove child profiles and local child photos using the app. You may also ask us to provide, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, or otherwise exercise rights available under UK data-protection law. Some information may need to be retained where the law requires it or where necessary to establish or defend legal claims.

International processing

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used by those providers for international transfers.

Security

We use account authentication, access controls and server-side checks designed to protect Moonbeam data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so parents should avoid entering information that is not needed for story personalisation.

Changes

We may update this policy as Moonbeam develops. The date at the top will show when it was last revised.
