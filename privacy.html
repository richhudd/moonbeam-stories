Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

A note to parents

Moonbeam is built around something especially personal: your family and, if you choose, photographs of your children. We understand that trusting an online service with this information is a significant decision.

Your photographs remain yours. Uploading a photograph to Moonbeam does not transfer ownership of the photograph, or ownership of your child's image or likeness, to Moonbeam. We use photographs only as described in this policy to provide the Moonbeam services you ask us to provide.

We do not sell your family photographs. We do not make them publicly available. We do not use them to advertise Moonbeam without your separate permission.

Who we are

Moonbeam Stories is currently operated in the United Kingdom by the individual trading as Moonbeam Stories. For privacy questions or requests about personal information, contact privacy@moonbeamstories.co.uk.

Who Moonbeam is for

Moonbeam is a parent-controlled service. Accounts and purchases are for adults, and children are not expected to create or manage their own Moonbeam accounts. Please provide only the information reasonably needed to personalise your family's stories and do not enter unnecessary sensitive information about a child.

Information we use

Depending on how you use Moonbeam, we may process the parent's email address and account identifiers; names or nicknames, ages, interests and story preferences entered for children; photographs voluntarily uploaded for character personalisation; generated and saved stories and illustrations; story-credit and transaction records; and limited technical and security information needed to operate and protect the service.

Your family photographs

Adding a photograph is optional. If you add one to a saved child profile, Moonbeam stores a resized reference copy in private account storage so it can be used when you request personalised illustrations. The app may also keep a local cached copy on your device. When an illustration needs to resemble the child, the reference image is securely provided to our AI image provider for that generation. You retain your rights in photographs you upload. Moonbeam receives only the permission reasonably necessary to store and process them for the services you request. Removing the photograph or deleting the child profile removes the corresponding private reference copy used by Moonbeam; illustrations already created from it may remain in stories you generated or saved.

Why we use personal information

We use account and transaction information where necessary to provide the service requested by the adult account holder, including accounts, purchases, credits and saved stories. We use child-profile information and optional photographs to provide the personalised story and illustration features the parent requests. We also process limited information where necessary for legitimate interests such as securing Moonbeam, preventing fraud and abuse, diagnosing technical failures and maintaining a reliable service, while giving particular weight to children's interests and rights. We may retain or process information where necessary to comply with legal obligations.

Companies that help us provide Moonbeam

Moonbeam currently uses OpenAI for AI story, illustration and narration generation; Supabase for authentication, database and application storage; Stripe for payments; Resend for account emails; and Vercel for hosting and server-side functions. They receive information only where necessary for their part in providing or securing the service and process it under their applicable contractual, privacy and security arrangements. OpenAI states that data submitted through its API is not used to train its models by default.

Payments

Payment-card details are entered directly into Stripe Checkout. Moonbeam does not store your full card details. We receive information such as payment status, transaction identifiers and the information needed to allocate and administer story credits.

Fraud and abuse prevention

Moonbeam may use limited technical information to protect accounts and prevent misuse, including repeated claims of introductory offers. For the introductory offer, Moonbeam uses pseudonymous hashes derived from browser/device and network information. Raw IP addresses and raw device identifiers are not stored in the introductory-trial claims table. These signals are used for security and abuse prevention, not advertising.

How long we keep information

Account information, saved profiles, saved stories, credit and transaction information are retained for as long as needed to provide the service and, where appropriate, afterwards for accounting, security, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Reference photographs may be retained while the corresponding saved profile exists. Device-cached copies can remain until the photograph is removed, browser data is cleared or device storage is otherwise deleted. We do not keep personal information for longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is held.

International processing

Some companies that provide Moonbeam's infrastructure may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, appropriate transfer arrangements are used.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable UK data-protection law, you may have rights to ask for access to personal information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, or to object to certain processing. You can also remove child profiles and their stored reference photographs within Moonbeam. These rights are not absolute in every circumstance; for example, some records may have to be retained to comply with law or establish or defend legal claims. Contact privacy@moonbeamstories.co.uk to exercise a right or ask a privacy question. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how your personal information has been handled.

Security

We use authentication, private storage, access controls and server-side safeguards designed to protect Moonbeam information. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so we also encourage parents not to provide information that is unnecessary for creating their stories.

Changes to this policy

If Moonbeam changes materially, we may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
