Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

A note to parents

Moonbeam is built around something especially personal: your family and, if you choose, photographs of your children. We understand that trusting an online service with this information is a significant decision.

Your photographs remain yours. Uploading a photograph to Moonbeam does not transfer ownership of the photograph, or ownership of your child’s image or likeness, to Moonbeam. We use photographs only as described in this policy to provide the Moonbeam services you ask us to provide.

We do not sell your family photographs. We do not make them publicly available. We do not use them to advertise Moonbeam without your separate permission.

Who we are

Moonbeam Stories is currently operated in the United Kingdom by the individual trading as Moonbeam Stories. For privacy questions, contact privacy@moonbeamstories.co.uk.

Who Moonbeam is for

Moonbeam is a parent-controlled service. Accounts and purchases are for adults, and children are not expected to create or manage their own Moonbeam accounts. Please provide only the information reasonably needed to personalise your family’s stories and avoid entering unnecessary sensitive information about a child.

Information we use

Depending on how you use Moonbeam, we may process the parent’s email address and account identifiers; child-profile details entered by the parent; optional reference photographs; generated and saved stories and illustrations; story-credit and transaction records; and limited technical and security information needed to operate and protect the service.

Your family photographs

Adding a photograph is optional. If you add a photograph to a saved child profile, Moonbeam stores a resized reference copy in private account storage so it can be used when you request personalised illustrations. A local cached copy may also be stored on your device. When an illustration needs to resemble the child, the reference image is securely provided to our AI service provider for that generation.

You retain your rights in photographs you upload. Moonbeam receives only the limited permission necessary to store and process them to provide the services you request. Removing a photograph or deleting its child profile removes the corresponding private reference copy used by Moonbeam. Illustrations already created from that photograph may remain in stories you have generated or saved.

Why we use personal information

We use information where necessary to provide the Moonbeam service requested by the account holder, including creating and authenticating accounts, generating and saving stories, producing personalised illustrations and narration, and managing purchases and story credits. We also process limited information where necessary for legitimate interests such as securing Moonbeam, preventing fraud and abuse, diagnosing technical failures and maintaining the reliability of the service. Where information relates to a child, we give particular weight to the child’s interests and rights. We may also retain or process information where necessary to comply with a legal obligation.

AI processing

Moonbeam uses OpenAI’s API to create stories, illustrations and narration. Information needed for the requested generation may therefore be sent to OpenAI. OpenAI states that, by default, inputs and outputs submitted through its API are not used to train or improve its models unless the API customer explicitly opts in to data sharing.

Companies that help us provide Moonbeam

Moonbeam currently uses OpenAI for AI generation; Supabase for authentication, database and application storage; Stripe for payments; Resend for account emails; and Vercel for hosting and server-side functions. They receive information where necessary for their part in providing or securing the service and process it subject to their applicable contractual, privacy and security arrangements.

Payments

Payment-card details are entered directly into Stripe Checkout. Moonbeam does not store your full card details. We receive information such as payment status, transaction identifiers and the information needed to allocate and administer story credits.

Fraud and abuse prevention

Moonbeam may use limited technical information to protect accounts and prevent misuse, including repeated claims of introductory offers. For the introductory offer, Moonbeam uses pseudonymous hashes derived from browser/device and network information. Raw device identifiers and raw IP addresses are not stored in the introductory-trial claims table. These signals are used for security and abuse prevention, not advertising.

How long we keep information

Account information, saved profiles, saved stories, credit and transaction information are retained for as long as needed to provide the service and, where appropriate, afterwards for accounting, security, dispute resolution and legal obligations. Reference photographs may be retained while the corresponding saved profile exists. Device-cached copies can remain until the photograph or browser data is removed. We do not keep personal information for longer than reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it is held.

International processing

Some companies that provide Moonbeam’s infrastructure may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data-protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, appropriate transfer arrangements are used.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable UK data-protection law, you may have rights to ask for access to personal information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or data portability, or to object to certain processing. These rights are not absolute in every circumstance; for example, some records may need to be retained to comply with law or establish or defend legal claims. You can contact privacy@moonbeamstories.co.uk about these rights.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office if you are unhappy with how your personal information has been handled.

Security

We use authentication, private storage, access controls and server-side safeguards designed to protect Moonbeam information. No online system can guarantee absolute security, so we also encourage parents not to provide information that is unnecessary for creating their stories.

Changes to this policy

If Moonbeam changes materially, we may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
