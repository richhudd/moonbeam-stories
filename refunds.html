Refund Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

Moonbeam Stories sells prepaid story credits used to create personalised digital stories. This policy applies to credits purchased directly from Moonbeam Stories through Stripe.

Change of mind

We do not offer a separate goodwill or money-back guarantee simply because you change your mind. Where applicable law gives you a cancellation right, we will of course honour it. Before Moonbeam begins the first story made from each newly purchased credit pack, we ask you to expressly agree to immediate digital supply and acknowledge the effect beginning that digital supply has on cancellation.

Once digital content has been supplied

Where you have expressly requested immediate supply and acknowledged the relevant cancellation-right consequences, the statutory 14-day cancellation right may end once supply of the digital content has begun. A successfully generated story is therefore not normally refundable merely because you dislike its creative style, wording or illustrations. This does not affect any remedy you may have if the content or service is faulty, not as described, or otherwise fails to meet legal requirements.

Technical failures

If a technical failure consumes a story credit but Moonbeam does not successfully provide the story, our normal remedy is to restore the credit automatically. If that does not happen, contact us and we will investigate. Where the law requires a refund or another remedy, we will provide it.

Unused credits

Purchased credits do not currently expire. Unused credits are not automatically refundable outside any cancellation or other refund right that applies under law. Promotional or introductory credits have no cash value and are not refundable.

Refunds required by law

Nothing in this policy limits your statutory rights. Where you are legally entitled to a refund, we will process it without undue delay and in accordance with the applicable legal requirements.

How to contact us

Email support@moonbeamstories.co.uk from the email address used for your Moonbeam account. Include the approximate purchase date and the credit pack purchased. Do not send card details.

Processing approved refunds

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method unless another method is lawfully agreed. Banks and card providers may take additional time to display a refund after Stripe processes it.
