Refund Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Moonbeam sells prepaid story credits used to create personalised digital stories. This policy applies to credits purchased directly from Moonbeam through Stripe.

Changing your mind

We do not offer an additional goodwill money-back guarantee simply because you change your mind. This does not remove any cancellation or refund right you have under consumer law.

Where required, before Moonbeam begins supplying digital content from a newly purchased credit pack, we ask you to expressly agree to immediate supply and acknowledge the effect this may have on your cancellation rights.

After story generation begins

Where the legal requirements for immediate digital supply have been satisfied, the normal 14-day cancellation right may cease once supply of the digital content begins. A successfully supplied personalised story is therefore not normally refundable simply because you dislike a creative choice in its wording or illustrations.

That does not affect your rights where the digital content or service is faulty, not as described or otherwise does not meet the requirements of consumer law.

Failed generations

If a technical failure consumes a story credit but Moonbeam does not successfully provide the story, Moonbeam’s normal response is to restore the credit automatically. If this does not happen, contact support@moonbeamstories.co.uk and we will investigate. Where the law entitles you to a refund or another remedy, that legal right takes precedence over this policy.

Unused credits

Purchased story credits currently do not expire. Unused credits are not automatically refundable except where a cancellation, refund or other remedy is required by law. Promotional and introductory credits have no cash value and cannot be refunded.

How refunds are paid

Where a refund is due, it will normally be returned through the original payment method. The bank or card provider may take additional time to show the refund after it has been processed.

Contact us

Email support@moonbeamstories.co.uk from the email address associated with your Moonbeam account and include the approximate purchase date. Never send us your full card details.
