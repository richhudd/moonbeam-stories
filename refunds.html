Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

We want the prepaid-credit model to be straightforward. This policy applies to story credits purchased directly from Moonbeam Stories through Stripe.

Unused purchased credits

If you change your mind, contact us within 14 days of purchase. We will normally refund the value of purchased credits that remain unused from that purchase. If part of the pack has already been used, we may deduct the value attributable to stories already generated, subject always to your statutory rights.

Faulty or unavailable service

If a technical failure consumes a credit but Moonbeam does not successfully provide the story, contact us. Where the system has not already restored the credit automatically, we can investigate and, where appropriate, restore the credit or provide a refund.

Generated stories

Because AI-generated stories and illustrations are personalised and generation incurs an immediate service cost, a story that has been successfully generated will not normally be refundable merely because of subjective preferences about its creative style. This does not affect remedies that may apply where the service is faulty, not as described, or where consumer law otherwise requires a remedy.

Free credits

Introductory or promotional credits have no cash value and are not refundable.

How to request a refund

Email support@moonbeamstories.co.uk from the email address used for your Moonbeam account. Include the approximate purchase date and the credit pack purchased. Do not send card details.

Processing

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method. Banks and card providers may take additional time to display a refund after Stripe processes it.

Statutory rights

This policy is intended to sit alongside, not replace or restrict, rights available to consumers under applicable law.
