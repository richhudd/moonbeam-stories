HOW IT WORKS

Their world becomes the Cast. Their imagination starts the adventure.

Create a Cast from the children, trusted adults and pets in their world, then turn an idea into an original illustrated adventure to enjoy together.

1

Create their Cast

Add a child with just a name and age. Trusted adults and pets can join too. A photograph is completely optional.

2

Imagine an adventure

Give Moonbeam an idea of your own, or let it dream up tonight's story for you.

3

Read or listen together

Moonbeam creates an age-appropriate illustrated story to read like a book, hear narrated and save for another night.

STORIES THAT LOOK LIKE THEM

A familiar face. A completely new adventure.

If you choose to add a photo, Moonbeam can keep your child recognisable from the cover to the final page — an astronaut tonight, a woodland explorer tomorrow.

No photo? No problem. Moonbeam works without one too.

YOUR FAMILY IS PERSONAL

We treat it that way.

Adding a photograph is optional. If you choose to add one, it is used to help create illustrations that resemble your child.

♡

Your photos remain yours

Uploading a photo does not give Moonbeam ownership of it or of your child's image or likeness.

◌

Private by design

We do not sell your family photographs, make them public or use them to advertise Moonbeam without separate permission.

✓

You're in control

You can use Moonbeam without a photograph and remove saved reference photos from your child profiles.
